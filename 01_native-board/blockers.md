## Applying the useful structure
Retain Blocker ID, Layer, Description, Impact, Status and Next Check where a chosen view needs them. For actual use, bind the subject and source version, precise blocked action and resolution condition. A next-check date is not a schedule trigger. Readable evidence or a peer's receipt alone does not resolve the obstacle.

Hold only actions depending on an unresolved safety, source or authority condition. Do not freeze every external layer, create replacement rules, open subscriptions or wait for every board to acknowledge a local blocker. Record material changes with their real observation time and source; no mandatory duplicate log per unchanged check.
